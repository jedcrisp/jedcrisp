Privacy Policy for Average Dad Athletics

Last Updated: January 2025

Thank you for using Average Dad Athletics. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our fitness and training platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you use Average Dad Athletics, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, and profile information when you create an account
  • Fitness Data: Workout logs, exercise data, sets, reps, weights, training programs, goals, and progress tracking information
  • Health Information: Body measurements, weight, body fat percentage, and other health metrics you choose to track
  • Community Content: Posts, comments, photos, and other content you share in the community features
  • Preferences: App settings, notification preferences, and customization options

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use Average Dad Athletics, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent in the app
  • Technical Data: IP address, crash reports, performance data, and error logs
  • Location Data: If you enable location services for features like finding nearby gyms or tracking outdoor workouts

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our fitness and training services
  • Enable cloud synchronization of your workout data across your devices
  • Personalize your experience with customized training programs and recommendations
  • Generate progress reports, analytics, and insights about your fitness journey
  • Facilitate community features, including connecting with other users
  • Send you notifications about workouts, achievements, and community updates
  • Respond to your requests, comments, and questions
  • Send you technical notices, updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and unauthorized access

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

Your fitness data and account information are stored securely using cloud-based services. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data both in transit and at rest. Your data is backed up regularly to ensure availability and recovery.

3.2 Security Measures

Average Dad Athletics employs robust security measures to protect your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive health and fitness data
  • Secure authentication and authorization systems
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Compliance with industry security standards
  • Access controls to ensure only authorized users can view your data

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information or fitness data to third parties.

4.1 Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers
  • Analytics providers (to understand app usage and improve our services)
  • Payment processors (for subscription and in-app purchases)
  • Email service providers (for sending notifications and updates)

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and only use it for the services they provide.

4.2 Community Features

When you use community features, your profile information, posts, and comments may be visible to other users. You can control the visibility of your profile and content through your privacy settings.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access and review your personal information and fitness data at any time within the app
  • Update: You can modify or update your information and preferences through the app settings
  • Delete: You can delete your account and all associated data through the app settings or by contacting us
  • Export: You can export your fitness data at any time
  • Privacy Settings: You can control who can see your profile and fitness data through privacy settings

6. Third-Party Services

Average Dad Athletics may integrate with third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Apple HealthKit / Google Fit: If you choose to sync with health platforms, your data is subject to their privacy policies
  • Payment Processors: For subscription and in-app purchases, payment information is handled by secure third-party processors
  • Analytics Services: We may use analytics services to understand app usage and improve our services

7. Children's Privacy

Average Dad Athletics is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside your country of residence. By using Average Dad Athletics, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or business purposes.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, by posting a notice in the app or sending you an email.

11. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information held by us and our service providers
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights
