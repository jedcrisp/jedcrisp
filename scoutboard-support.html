Scoutboard Support

Help and contact information for Scoutboard, the job tracking web app.

Contact Support

For help with Scoutboard, email support with a short description of the issue and any steps needed to reproduce it.

Email: jed@chimeo.com

Support URL: https://jedcrisp.com/scoutboard-support.html

What to Include

  • The email address associated with your Scoutboard account, if applicable.
  • The browser and device you were using.
  • A description of what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Screenshots or error messages, if available.

Common Help Topics

  • Creating and updating companies, job roles, application statuses, and notes.
  • Managing follow-ups, interview details, and job posting links.
  • Account access, login issues, and data deletion requests.
  • Privacy questions about job search data stored in Scoutboard.
